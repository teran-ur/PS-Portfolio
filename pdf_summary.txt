5 Rules for Putting a Caller on Hold
Dealing with Angry / Upset Callers
1. Ask permission before putting them on
hold ("May I put you on hold?").
2. Wait for their response before
pressing hold (never just set the handset
down!).
3. Explain why they are being placed on
hold.
4. Give a time frame (keep holds under
30–45 seconds; if longer, offer to call
back).
5. Thank them for holding as soon as
you return.
• Remember they are mad at the issue,
not at you personally.
• Show sincere empathy: "I'm sorry about
that; tell me what happened."
• Do not interrupt, challenge the
complaint, or blame others.
• Take careful notes, repeat their name
and number, and commit to a clear
solution within 1 working day.
